## Capture list for the recording

- **Shot 2, The virtual desk:** Workstation windows on three virtual monitors. Over-the-shoulder needs a second camera or a mixed-reality capture.
- **Shot 3, Flat windows:** Open the 2D board in a normal Quest Browser window (no AR). Hold still for 4 s.
- **Shot 5, Pin it to the wall:** Enter AR → Wall → aim at a clear wall → trigger. Include the ghost board and the scan sweep.
- **Shot 6, Wall, desk, float:** Three placements: Wall, Desk with a tilt drag on the hinge, Float beside a virtual monitor.
- **Shot 7, Still there tomorrow:** Exit the session, re-enter on the same wall. Frame it like shot 5 so the cut matches.
- **Shot 8, Pick it up:** Close to the board. Pinch the Safari login note in In progress, carry it slowly to Done. Hold on the confetti.
- **Shot 9, Tear off, throw away:** Tear-off: grab and pull more than 12 cm, release in mid-air. Archive: tear off another and flick it down.
- **Shot 10, Just say it:** Press Voice on the board and say the line clearly. Keep the room audio, it plays in the final cut.
- **Shot 11, Write on it:** Whiteboard skin: take the red marker from the tray, circle one note, draw a short arrow.
- **Shot 12, It moves for the team too:** Same card move as shot 8, plus a screen capture of the Workstation board window (or a real synced project) updating.
- **Shot 13, One board, every screen:** Short clips or screen captures of each platform. For 2D: Tab to a card, Space, arrow keys to move it.
- **Shot 14, Pull back:** Wide shot of the full setup. Over-the-shoulder like shot 2 if we have the second camera; otherwise a slow POV step back.

Record on Quest 3 with passthrough, Whiteboard skin and the demo board. Hold each action 2 s before and after, move your head slowly, and keep clean room audio for the voice command in shot 10.
