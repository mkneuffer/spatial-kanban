# Spatial Kanban trailer: storyboard (draft 2)

1:24, 16:9, 1080p, 30 fps. **Part 1** (0:00–0:30) is a single 30-second take of the app in action, with music only. **Part 2** (0:30–1:24) is narrated and explains why the product exists and how it works, with short clips cut in. Sources: **REC** = app recording, **PROP** = Workstation windows (`trailer.html`), **GFX** = type and titles made in the edit.

## Capture list

- **Part 1, one take (30 s):** enter ar, pin the board → move cards by hand → tear off, park, throw → say it → pull back.
- **2.2 Workstations are going virtual:** Workstation windows on three virtual monitors. The over-the-shoulder angle needs a second camera or a mixed-reality capture.
- **2.3 Still flat:** Open the 2D board in a normal Quest Browser window, outside AR. Hold still for 4 seconds.
- **2.5 Wall, desk or mid-air:** Three placements: Wall; Desk with a tilt drag on the hinge; Float next to a virtual monitor.
- **2.6 There tomorrow:** Exit the session, re-enter and face the same wall. Frame it like Part 1 beat 1 so the cut matches.
- **2.7 Cards are objects:** Lean in close and peel a note slowly toward the lens. Different angle from Part 1 beat 2.
- **2.8 Or just say it:** Re-use the voice take, or record a tighter one framed on the board.
- **2.9 It moves for your team too:** The card move on the wall, plus a screen capture of the Workstation board window, or a real synced project, updating.
- **2.10 One board, every screen:** A 2-second clip or screen capture on each platform. For the 2D board: Tab to a card, press Space, move it with the arrow keys.

Record on Quest 3 with passthrough, using the Whiteboard skin and the demo board. Move your head slowly. For Part 2 clips, hold 2 s before and after each action. Record the voice command with clean room audio.
